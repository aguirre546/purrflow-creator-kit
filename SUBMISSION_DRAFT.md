## Suggested write-up

Creators often repeat the same preparation work for every social platform: shaping a story, making a short video, rewriting captions, and organizing the next week. PurrFlow Creator Kit brings that work into one browser workspace.

The demo follows Mochi, an original cat character. A creator chooses one of three illustrated story templates, edits its title, hook, setup and payoff, and chooses a playful or cozy voice. Local writing rules prepare distinct copy for Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X and TikTok. English and Taglish settings adapt the surrounding caption copy; they do not translate the creator's story text.

The app animates a silent 15-second, 720 × 1280 vertical short in the browser, with timed narrative captions. It renders a downloadable video using the browser's supported format, preferring MP4 and falling back to WebM. The creator can export an SRT, build a seven-day plan at three to five stories per day, and download a ZIP containing the copy, story, caption timing, plan, workspace backup and rendered video. Saved browser workspaces and portable JSON backups help the creator return to a draft.

We rebuilt the entry around a complete creation and preparation experience that works without social account credentials or paid generation APIs. The key craftsmanship is the path from a brief to a previewable short and portable materials: consistent story details, distinct platform copy, readable timed text and an inspectable weekly plan.

The app makes template-based animation, rather than generating arbitrary scenes with an AI model. The creator publishes or schedules the exported video through the social platforms. A posting target in the plan is not a scheduled social post. The seven-day plan is not a claim that all its videos have been rendered or that the app runs unattended for seven days. Suggested copy uses local templates and the supplied brief; there is no connected language-model API in this build. WebM may require conversion for the chosen publishing destination. Rendered video is not retained in browser workspace saves, so creators must download the video or ZIP before closing the tab.

Victor Aguirre built this solo with AI coding assistance from Codex. The Mochi reference artwork and the separate provided AI sample are AI generated; the sample video was created using existing Google Flow credits. Newly rendered videos use an original procedural cat illustration, without calling Flow or another generation service. Platform names identify intended export destinations and do not imply affiliation. The rebuilt app does not collect social login credentials or publish automatically.
