## Why rebuild

The original unattended Flow/social system depended on unfinished provider authorization, verification, credit eligibility and always-on processing. Rewriting that integration layer would not remove provider requirements. This rebuild implements a smaller complete workflow that can be demonstrated today: a story becomes a procedural short, five editable captions and portable files.

This is a separate implementation, not a claim that the original integration problems were fixed. The earlier project and public beta are preserved. The original five-platform launch used native publishing tools and remains separate evidence.

## Honest boundaries

Three fixed illustrated plots; story text does not generate arbitrary new scenes. The seven-day plan contains editorial variations, not 21–35 unique finished videos. Rendering is silent and approximately fifteen seconds; codec/frame timing depend on the browser. AI sample playback is a provided reference, not a live Flow connection. Publishing and actual social scheduling use platform tools separately. Existing-credit generation and unattended publishing remain future integration work.
