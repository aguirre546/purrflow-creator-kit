## Prepared demonstration brief

- Story: Mochi receives a new plush cat bed. He sniffs it, notices the shipping box, squeezes into the box, and looks pleased.
- Voice: Playful.
- Caption language: English. Taglish changes the surrounding copy, not the entered story.
- Hook: “We bought the perfect bed. Mochi chose the packaging.”
- Setup and payoff: use the bed-versus-box preset and retain its matching visual structure.
- Animation: the preset's original procedural cat scene with narrative text, rendered in this browser.
- Separate optional reference: the existing 15-second Google Flow sample, clearly labeled as provided AI content. Do not substitute it for evidence of newly rendered output.

## Rehearsal checks

- The whole walkthrough fits three minutes, including download time.
- Every line of copy and caption agrees with the chosen illustrated template; no invented dialogue.
- The timeline remains readable on a projector and fits the video duration.
- The seven-day plan has 21 or 35 slots for the selected setting, with no claim of generated video inventory.
- The exported package opens and contains the exact materials shown, including the newly rendered video. Render before export; the separate AI sample is excluded.
- Browser workspace saves exclude rendered video. Download the video or ZIP before closing the tab. WebM may require conversion before publishing on a destination platform.
- Refresh and recovery behavior match the final app. Keep an exported package available if the event network fails; disclose any switch to a local build.
- No API connection, publishing result, production schedule, score or seven-day reliability claim is implied by the demo.
